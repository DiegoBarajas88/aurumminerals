The thesis

Why mineral assets, why now

Gold trades near historic highs while copper demand outruns supply on the back of electrification. Yet medium-scale producing assets in Latin America remain priced far below their replacement value — starved of institutional capital and governance.

That is the gap Aurum occupies: we bring the technical teams, the legal structure and the operating discipline; our partners bring capital that wants real, cash-generating assets behind it.

  • Producing or near-production assets — not grassroots speculation
  • Verified titles, audited operations, traceable gold
  • Diligence before capital: geology, law and community
  • Aurum invests and operates alongside its partners
