Submit a project

Your project, evaluated seriously.

Aurum reviews a limited number of mining projects each year across Latin America — gold, copper and coal. If your asset is real, licensed and well documented, we want to see it.

Technical screening · NDA before data · Response in 10 business days
What we look for

Clear criteria. No exceptions.

  • —Minerals: gold, copper or coal.
  • —Region: Latin America.
  • —Legal: valid mining title — or an advanced application with a verifiable file number before the mining authority.
  • —Stage: advanced exploration, development or production.
  • —Ownership: we deal with title holders, empowered representatives, or brokers with written authorization.

What we don't evaluate

  • ✕Projects without a mining title or legal surface access.
  • ✕Intermediaries without written authorization from the title holder.
  • ✕Minerals outside our focus, or assets outside Latin America.
  • ✕"Opportunities" without a single verifiable document.

This protects your time and ours. Projects that don't meet the criteria today are kept in our registry for the future.
